imu_data_x 
Task: Acceleration + Angular rate (Raw data)
Type: geometry_msgs/msg/Accel

imu_gravity_data_x
Task: Gravity Vector (internal calculation, play around with this)
Type: gemoetry_msgs/msg/Vector3

imu_gyrobias_data_x
Task: Gyroscope Offset
Type: geometry_msgs/msg/Vector3

imu_rotation_data_x 
Task: Segment 3D rotation
Type: geometry_msgs/msg/Quaternion

mag_data_x (measuring earths magnetic field)
Task: Magnetometer field vector (Spikes von 2^8 und 2^16 rausfiltern)
Type: geometry_msgs/msg/Vector3

BOTH magnetic and imu measure rotation
Magnetic sensors data comes in less frequent (~1kHz max while IMU can go up to 8kHz)
